How to play

Guess the hidden word in six tries.

Every guess must be a valid five-letter word. Press Enter to submit. The tiles change after each guess to show how close you were.

C R A N E

R, N, and E are in the word but in different spots. C and A are not.

  • A Right spot — the letter is in the right place.
  • B Wrong spot — the letter is in the word elsewhere.
  • C Not in the word — the letter does not appear.

Each letter in the answer is claimed at most once. Repeated letters can turn gray even when the letter appears elsewhere.

A fresh daily puzzle arrives at local midnight. Practice rounds are unlimited and never touch your daily stats.
